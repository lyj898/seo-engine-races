Frequently asked questions

How do you verify each race?+

Every race listed here is cross-checked against multiple independent sources -- official websites, aggregators, and other public records -- before it's published. Each entry carries a reliability score reflecting how thoroughly its details have been verified, and a last-updated date showing when that check last happened.

How often is this data refreshed?+

Listings are re-checked on a regular schedule (roughly every 7 days) against their original sources, and updated automatically when something changes. If a fact can no longer be verified, its reliability score drops rather than the old information being kept silently.
